Pedestrian Infrastructure Gap Analysis at MARTA Bus Stops

In Progress

Spatial analysis of pedestrian infrastructure around 875 MARTA bus stops in DeKalb County. Combines OpenStreetMap sidewalk/crosswalk data, GDOT crash records, and Census ACS demographics to build a Pedestrian Access Score (PAS) for each stop. Finds 30% of stops have poor or no pedestrian infrastructure, with gaps disproportionately concentrated in majority-Black neighborhoods (r = −0.49). Population-normalized crash analysis reveals the PAS–crash relationship disappears after controlling for density, suggesting suppressed pedestrian demand at infrastructure-poor stops.
